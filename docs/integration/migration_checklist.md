| Series lifecycle manifest (live started/completed/failed/outcome tracking) | done, with a confirmed persistence gap | `SeriesStorage` tracks `requested_experiments`/`experiments_started`/`experiments_completed`/`experiments_failed` and a `finalized_outcome`/`finalized_utc`, written atomically into `status.json` on every transition — this part is real, reliable persistence, not merely in-memory. **But**: `status.json` is write-only in this codebase (confirmed by `grep -rl "status.json" src/` — only `experiment_storage.py` itself touches it); nothing ever reads it back.
